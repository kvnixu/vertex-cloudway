Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app and look for Restore Purchases in its settings or store screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On iPhone or iPad, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. On Mac, open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings → Subscriptions.

I found a bug. What should I do?

Email us with the details listed above. Bug reports with steps to reproduce help us fix things fastest.

How do I ask for my data to be deleted?

Email contact@vertexcloudway.com with the subject "Data Request". See our Privacy Policy for details on what we collect.

Can I suggest a feature?

Yes, please! Email us or share it in our Discord. We read every idea.
