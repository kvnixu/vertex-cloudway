Privacy Policy

Vertex Cloudway LLC ("Vertex Cloudway," "we," "us") builds apps for Apple platforms. We collect as little data as possible. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Scope

This policy applies to vertexcloudway.com and to all apps published by Vertex Cloudway LLC on the App Store, including Kitty Cloud, AutoRest, Ophelia, Maze Runner and Agent X. If an app has its own privacy notice, that notice adds app-specific details to this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

If you email us, join our Discord or ask for support, we receive what you send: for example your name, email address and the content of your message.

Information stored on your device

Most app content, such as game progress, settings and journal entries, is stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud, that content may sync through your own iCloud account. We cannot see it.

Diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. We use these only to fix bugs and improve performance.

Website

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type for security and operations. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site trackers on our website.

3. How we use information

  • To provide and run our apps and website
  • To answer your questions and support requests
  • To fix bugs, prevent abuse and improve our products
  • To meet legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

4. How information is shared

We share information only when needed:

  • Service providers who help us run our business (for example, email and hosting), under obligations to protect it
  • Legal reasons, when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others
  • Business transfers, if Vertex Cloudway is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets

5. Apple services

Our apps may use Apple services such as the App Store, In-App Purchase, iCloud and Game Center. Apple processes payments, and we never receive your payment card details. Apple's handling of your data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Data retention and security

We keep support emails and messages only as long as needed to help you and for our records, then delete them. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the equivalent minimum age where you live. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or state privacy laws in the U.S. such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, email contact@vertexcloudway.com with the subject "Privacy Request". We will respond within the time required by law.

You can also delete an app to remove its on-device data, and turn off app analytics sharing in your device's privacy settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
